# Codex++ 1.0.12

Streaming replies and other chat updates no longer make the Settings integration search the conversation or measure its layout. Codex++ watches for the native Settings dialog to open and releases its content observer when the dialog closes.

Config, Tweaks, Tweak Store, and registered pages appear when you open Settings from the menu or keyboard shortcut. They also stay available when an older full-screen Settings layout replaces its sidebar. If you cancel Back to app, your selected page stays open.

Update with `codexplusplus update` and restart Codex.
